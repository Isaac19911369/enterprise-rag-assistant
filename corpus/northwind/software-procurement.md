# Software and SaaS Procurement

## Approved software catalogue
Software from the approved catalogue can be installed through the Company Portal without approval. The catalogue includes common developer tools, browsers, Python and Node.js runtimes and the Microsoft 365 apps.

## Requesting new software
Software that is not in the catalogue is requested through the Service Desk with the category "Software request". Every request is reviewed by IT Operations and Information Security; the review takes up to 10 business days.

## SaaS and cloud services
New SaaS tools that process Internal or Confidential data additionally require a vendor security assessment and a data processing agreement. Purchases above EUR 10,000 per year require a quote from at least two vendors.

## Open-source licences
Open-source components with permissive licences (MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0) can be used freely in products. Copyleft licences such as GPL or AGPL require approval from the Legal team before use in any product that is shipped to customers.
