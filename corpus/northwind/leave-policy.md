# Leave and Absence Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per year, increasing to 30 days after 25 years of service in line with Austrian law. Leave is requested in the HR portal and approved by the manager. Unused leave can be carried over, but leave that is older than two years expires.

## Sick leave
If you are sick, inform your manager before your usual start time. A medical certificate is required from the fourth calendar day of absence, or from the first day if requested by People & Culture.

## Parental leave
Parents can take parental leave until the child's second birthday. Northwind tops up the statutory benefit with a bonus of one month's gross salary for every parent who takes at least two months of parental leave.

## Special leave
Paid special leave is granted for your own wedding (3 days), the birth of your child (2 days), moving house (1 day per year) and the death of a close family member (3 days).

## Sabbatical
After 5 years of service, employees can apply for an unpaid sabbatical of 1 to 6 months. Applications must be submitted at least 4 months in advance.
