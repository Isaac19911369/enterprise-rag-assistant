# Onboarding Guide

## Before your first day
You will receive your laptop by courier, together with two hardware keys. Activation instructions for your account arrive by SMS on the Friday before your start date.

## First week
Your first day starts at 09:00 with a welcome session in the Linz office or online. During the first week you complete the mandatory trainings: information security (45 minutes), data protection (30 minutes) and health and safety (20 minutes). Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from another team.

## Probation period
The probation period is one month, as defined in the Austrian employment law. A first feedback conversation with your manager takes place after 6 weeks and a goal-setting conversation after 3 months.

## Who to ask
For questions about payroll and contracts contact People & Culture at people@northwind.example. For accounts, laptops and access, use the Service Desk portal.
