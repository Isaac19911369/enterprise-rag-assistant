# Incident Management

## Severity levels
- **SEV1**: a customer-facing production system is down or data is at risk. Response within 15 minutes, 24/7.
- **SEV2**: major degradation of a production system, or an internal system that blocks many employees. Response within 1 hour during business hours.
- **SEV3**: limited impact with a workaround. Response within 1 business day.

## Raising an incident
Any employee can raise an incident in the Service Desk portal. SEV1 incidents must additionally be announced in the Teams channel "#incident-response", which pages the on-call engineer.

## On-call
The on-call rotation changes every Monday at 10:00. On-call engineers receive an allowance of EUR 250 per week plus compensation for time actively worked outside business hours.

## Post-incident review
A blameless post-incident review is mandatory for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident and must be published within 5 business days. Action items are tracked as tickets with an owner and a due date.
