# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and passphrases
All Northwind accounts must use a passphrase of at least 14 characters. Passphrases are not rotated on a fixed schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected. Reusing a Northwind passphrase on any external website is prohibited. The approved password manager is Bitwarden, provisioned automatically for every employee.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the HR portal and all cloud consoles. The preferred second factor is a FIDO2 hardware key; every employee receives two YubiKeys during onboarding. SMS codes are only allowed as a temporary fallback for a maximum of 7 days after a key is lost.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the Service Desk within 1 hour, by phone at extension 4357 or through the portal. The device will be remotely wiped. Reporting quickly is never penalised; delayed reporting may be.

## Phishing
Suspicious emails must be reported with the "Report Phish" button in Outlook. Do not forward them to colleagues. Information Security runs a simulated phishing campaign every quarter; employees who click a simulated link are enrolled in a 20-minute refresher training.
