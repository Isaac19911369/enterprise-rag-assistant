# Payroll Schedule and Procedures

## Payment dates
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is made on the previous working day. The 13th and 14th monthly salaries are paid with the June and November salaries.

## Cut-off
Changes to salary, bonuses and expense reimbursements must reach Payroll by the 10th of the month to be included in the same month's run.

## Bank file approval
The SEPA payment file is approved with the four-eyes principle by the Payroll Lead and the CFO or their deputy.
