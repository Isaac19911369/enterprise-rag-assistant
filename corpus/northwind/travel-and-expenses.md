# Travel and Expenses

## Booking travel
Business travel is booked through the travel portal (Navan) after the trip is approved by your manager. Trains are preferred for trips under 5 hours; flights for shorter distances need a written justification. Economy class is standard for all flights; business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Hotels
Hotels are booked through the travel portal. The nightly limit is EUR 150 in most cities and EUR 220 in Munich, Zurich, London and Paris.

## Per diem and meals
Meals during business travel are covered by a daily per diem rather than receipts: EUR 30 for a full day in Austria and Germany, and the official Austrian rate for other countries. If a meal is provided by the host or the hotel, the per diem is reduced by EUR 10 per meal.

## Submitting expenses
Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the end of the trip, with a photo of each receipt. Claims above EUR 500 require approval by the cost-centre owner in addition to the manager. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly salary run.
