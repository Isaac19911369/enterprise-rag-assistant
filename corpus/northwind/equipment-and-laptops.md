# Equipment and Laptops

## Standard equipment
Every employee receives a managed laptop, a docking station, two monitors for the office desk and a headset. Engineers can choose between a 14-inch ThinkPad with 64 GB RAM and a 14-inch MacBook Pro with 36 GB RAM. All other roles receive a 14-inch ThinkPad with 32 GB RAM.

## Replacement cycle
Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or earlier if a repair would cost more than 40% of the replacement price. Requests for an early replacement go through the Service Desk with the category "Hardware".

## Home office equipment
Employees with an approved home-office agreement can order one monitor, one keyboard and one mouse for home use. In addition, a one-time home-office allowance of EUR 300 is paid with the next salary for furniture such as a chair or desk; receipts are not required.

## Returning equipment
All equipment must be returned on the last working day. Laptops must not be wiped by the employee; IT wipes them after a retention check.
